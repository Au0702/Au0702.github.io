I am an undergraduate researcher in Electronic Science and Technology at Southeast University. My research centers on neutral-atom quantum computing, spanning quantum error correction, fault-tolerant architecture, and hardware-aware compilation.

I am currently working with Prof. Zhiding Liang at LIANG Lab and Prof. Yufei Ding at UC San Diego's PICASSO Lab. I enjoy turning physical constraints—from atom loss to transport limitations—into concrete compiler and architecture decisions.
